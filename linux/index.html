Linux, Containers & Automation

Linux has always been at the heart of what I do.

My experience covers enterprise Linux environments on Fedora, Debian and Red Hat, virtualization, containerization, container orchestration, infrastructure automation and distributed computing.

I run and maintain core infrastructure services: DNS with BIND, DHCP, LDAP and RADIUS for identity and access, and web servers like nginx and Caddy.

I enjoy building reliable, reproducible and maintainable systems using open standards and open-source technologies, while avoiding unnecessary complexity and vendor lock-in.

For me, infrastructure should be transparent, understandable and built to last.
