Work

I work with research institutes, universities and government organizations across the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany.

I design, build and maintain open, secure, high-performance and self-hosted infrastructure: from enterprise networks and data center fabrics to Linux platforms, core services and local AI.

I'm fortunate to work for an organization that gives me the freedom, resources and opportunity to design and build experimental environments on a relatively large scale.

That lets me test ideas at scale, challenge conventional approaches and explore technologies before they become mainstream. It's one of the things I enjoy most about my work.
