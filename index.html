01 / about me

🐧 Linux Fanatic | Network Engineer | Open Infrastructure & Local AI Enthusiast

Building the infrastructure that should already exist.

Based in Western Europe. I work with research institutes, universities and government organizations in the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany.

I design, build and maintain open, secure and self-hosted infrastructure. I believe in transparency, sustainability, digital sovereignty and technological independence.

My world is Linux, networking, distributed systems, automation, identity management and security. From low-level protocols to large-scale designs, I love understanding how complex systems work, and making them work better.

🌐

Enterprise Networking & Infrastructure

I specialize in enterprise and data center networking: LACP, OSPF, BGP, EVPN, VXLAN and MACsec, plus advanced Layer 2 and Layer 3 designs.

I build leaf-spine (Clos) fabrics with BGP EVPN and anycast gateways, running on SONiC, the open network platform.

My goal: scalable, resilient networks that combine proven principles with modern automation.

🖥️

Linux, Containers & Automation

Linux has always been at the heart of what I do.

I work with Fedora, Debian and Red Hat, virtualization, containers, orchestration and automation, and run core services like DNS (BIND), DHCP, LDAP, RADIUS, nginx and Caddy.

I build systems that are reliable, reproducible and easy to maintain, using open standards and open source, without needless complexity or vendor lock-in.

Infrastructure should be transparent, understandable and built to last.

Local AI & High-Performance Computing

I'm a strong advocate of local, self-hosted AI.

I'm especially excited about NVIDIA DGX Spark combined with Exo: several machines and GPUs working together to run powerful AI models locally.

AI shouldn't depend only on big cloud providers. Individuals, researchers and organizations should be able to run it themselves, and stay in control of their own data.

Experimentation, Research & Innovation

I'm lucky to work for an organization that gives me the freedom and resources to build experimental environments on a large scale, well beyond a typical lab.

That lets me try new network designs, distributed computing, high-performance infrastructure and AI platforms, often before they go mainstream. It's one of the best parts of my job.

I believe innovation comes from curiosity and hands-on experimenting. And sometimes the best way to understand a system is to break it, find out why, and rebuild it better.
