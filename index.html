About me

🐧 Linux Fanatic | Network Engineer | Open Infrastructure & Local AI Enthusiast

Building the infrastructure that should already exist.

Based in Western Europe, collaborating with research institutes, universities, and government organizations across the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany.

I'm passionate about designing, building, and maintaining open, secure, high-performance, and self-hosted infrastructure, with a strong belief in transparency, sustainability, digital sovereignty, and technological independence.

My world revolves around Linux, enterprise networking, distributed systems, automation, identity management, and modern security architectures. From low-level network protocols to large-scale infrastructure design, I enjoy understanding how complex systems work and, more importantly, how to make them work better.

🌐 Enterprise Networking & Infrastructure

With extensive experience in enterprise networking, I specialize in technologies such as LACP, OSPF, BGP, EVPN, VXLAN, VNI, and MACsec, along with advanced Layer 2 and Layer 3 network architectures.

I work with Mellanox/NVIDIA networking, high-performance Ethernet fabrics, enterprise switching, routing, network virtualization, and software-defined infrastructure.

I'm particularly interested in designing scalable, resilient networks that combine proven networking principles with modern automation and software-defined architectures.

🖥️ Linux, Containers & Automation

Linux has always been at the heart of what I do.

My experience covers enterprise Linux environments, virtualization, containerization, container orchestration, infrastructure automation, and distributed computing.

I enjoy building reliable, reproducible, and maintainable systems using open standards and open-source technologies, while avoiding unnecessary complexity and vendor lock-in.

For me, infrastructure should be transparent, understandable, and built to last.

🧠 Local AI & High-Performance Computing

I'm a strong advocate of local AI, self-hosted machine learning, and decentralized computing.

My interests include NVIDIA DGX platforms, DGX Spark, Mellanox networking, GPU acceleration, deep learning containers, and distributed AI workloads.

I'm particularly fascinated by the possibilities of combining NVIDIA DGX Spark with tools like Exo, exploring how multiple machines and GPUs can collaborate to run powerful AI models locally.

I believe the future of AI shouldn't depend entirely on centralized cloud infrastructure. Powerful AI should also be accessible to individuals, researchers, and organizations that value privacy, independence, and control over their own data.

🔬 Experimentation, Research & Innovation

I'm fortunate to work for an organization that gives me the freedom, resources, and opportunity to design and build experimental environments on a relatively large scale.

This allows me to explore emerging technologies, experiment with advanced networking architectures, distributed computing, high-performance infrastructure, and AI platforms in environments that go well beyond a traditional lab setup.

Having the opportunity to test ideas at scale, challenge conventional approaches, and explore technologies before they become mainstream is one of the things I enjoy most about my work.

I strongly believe that innovation comes from curiosity, hands-on experimentation, and the freedom to challenge established solutions.

And sometimes, the best way to understand a system is to break it, figure out why it failed, and rebuild it better.

Open Infrastructure. Secure Networks. Local Intelligence. Digital Independence.

Because the most interesting infrastructure isn't just the infrastructure you maintain. It's the infrastructure you get to imagine, design, build, and improve.
