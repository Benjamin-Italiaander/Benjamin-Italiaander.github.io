Local AI & High-Performance Computing

Powerful AI shouldn't depend entirely on centralized cloud infrastructure.

I'm a strong advocate of local AI, self-hosted machine learning and decentralized computing.

My interests include NVIDIA DGX platforms, DGX Spark, GPU acceleration, deep learning containers and distributed AI workloads.

I'm particularly fascinated by the possibilities of combining NVIDIA DGX Spark with tools like Exo, exploring how multiple machines and GPUs can collaborate to run powerful AI models locally.

I believe the future of AI shouldn't depend entirely on centralized cloud infrastructure. Powerful AI should also be accessible to individuals, researchers and organizations that value privacy, independence and control over their own data.
